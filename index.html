The mechanics

Four steps from claim to verdict

Every claim moves through the same transparent pipeline — anyone can inspect why a verdict landed where it did.

01

Claim posted

A user submits a factual claim worth checking — short, falsifiable, and public.

02

Evidence attached

Anyone can attach sources for or against the claim, each becoming its own scored item.

03

Community votes

Evidence gets rated solid / weak by the crowd, weighted by rater reputation.

04

Verdict computed

A deterministic scoring engine turns weighted evidence into a verdict — and updates everyone's reputation.

Built for developers

A codebase engineers actually enjoy reading

Deterministic by design, tested with property-based fuzzing, and split cleanly between pure logic and infrastructure.

⚙

Pure scoring core

Zero runtime dependencies, no I/O. A single score() entry point you can unit test in milliseconds.

☁

Cloudflare Workers

Hono + D1 + Better Auth, running at the edge. No servers to babysit.

∑

Deterministic math

Every accumulation is order-independent by construction — verified with property tests that shuffle input and assert bit-identical results.

◈

Two credibility strategies

Weighted-sum by default, with an ALS matrix-factorization strategy that kicks in automatically once voting density crosses a threshold.

🛡

Sybil resistance

Account-age gating and reputation weighting make brigading and vote farms expensive to pull off.

✓

Simulation-tested

Multi-cycle acceptance scenarios stress-test brigades, sybil farms, and bandwagon effects before they ship.
